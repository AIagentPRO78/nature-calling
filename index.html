Privacy Policy — Nature Calling

Last updated: 1 June 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Nature Calling application ("the App"), a personal tool used by the operator of the YouTube channel Nature Calling to publish the operator's own relaxing ambient videos to the operator's own Pinterest account.

What the App does

The App uses the Pinterest API solely to create and manage boards and Pins on the account of the App's own operator. It posts links to the operator's publicly available YouTube videos.

Information we access

When authorized, the App accesses, via the Pinterest API and only for the operator's own account:

  • Basic account information needed to authenticate the session.
  • The operator's boards and Pins, in order to create and organize them.

The App does not access, collect, or process information belonging to any other Pinterest user.

How information is used

Accessed information is used only to create boards and Pins on the operator's own account. It is used for no other purpose.

Data storage and sharing

  • The App stores its API access token locally on the operator's own computer to maintain the authorized session.
  • The App does not sell, rent, or share any information with third parties.
  • The App does not use accessed information for advertising or profiling.

Data retention

The locally stored access token is retained only while the App is in use and can be revoked at any time by the operator from their Pinterest account settings (Settings → Apps and connected accounts).

Third-party services

The App interacts with the Pinterest API (governed by Pinterest's own privacy policy) and links to YouTube (governed by Google's privacy policy). This policy covers only the App itself.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children and does not knowingly process any data relating to children.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed through the channel's contact options on its YouTube About page.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
